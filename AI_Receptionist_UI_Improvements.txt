AI Receptionist Demo UI Improvements

1. Improve the Welcome Message

Current: “Hi! I’m the AI Receptionist for Caps & Crowns Dental Clinic.”

Suggested: “Hi! 👋 Welcome to Caps & Crowns Dental Clinic. I’m your AI
receptionist. I can help you book, reschedule or cancel appointments,
answer questions about the clinic, and assist with dental emergencies.”

Reason: - More welcoming - Immediately communicates capabilities - Feels
more human

------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. Rename the Phone Number Input

Current: “Enter a phone number”

Suggested: “Patient Phone Number”

Helper text: “Used to simulate returning patients and persistent
conversation memory.”

Reason: Most users don’t understand why a phone number is required. This
explains the purpose.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. Add a “Powered By” Section

At the bottom of the sidebar add small technology badges:

• LangGraph • Django • PostgreSQL • Hybrid RAG • pgvector • Cal.com •
Twilio • Groq

Reason: Recruiters instantly understand the technical stack.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. Typing Indicator

While waiting for the backend:

“AI Receptionist is thinking…”

or animated typing dots.

Reason: Makes latency feel intentional and improves UX.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. Response Metadata

Display a small status bar below every AI response.

Example:

Workflow: FAQ Latency: 1.8 s Cache: HIT

Booking example:

Workflow: Booking Latency: 5.7 s

Reason: Shows this is a workflow-driven AI system instead of a basic
chatbot.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

6. Better Demo Scenarios

Add more scenario cards:

• Book appointment • Reschedule appointment • Cancel appointment • Show
current booking • Clinic address • Opening hours • Root canal
information • Emergency dental pain • Insurance query

Reason: Makes it easier to explore the application.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

7. Add Quick Action Chips

Below the welcome message:

[Book Appointment] [Reschedule] [Cancel] [Clinic Hours] [Clinic Address]

Reason: Improves discoverability.

Overall Goal

The UI should communicate that this is: - A production-style AI
application - Stateful - Workflow driven - Reliable - Built with modern
AI infrastructure

rather than appearing as just another chatbot.
